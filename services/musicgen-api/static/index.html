—PCS
Reading PCS: the share of voiced pitch within ±50 cents of the detected pentatonic scale, reported together with the voiced fraction (how much trackable melody there is). A higher PCS on less melody is not a win; the two numbers are read jointly. Real cassette recordings from the corpora score about 0.85–0.91 PCS at 0.43–0.53 voiced. n = 1 per click: this is a demonstration, not a measurement.
Rights: adapters were trained on corpora whose source audio is not distributed and is not served by this page. Everything you hear here is model output. research-only adapters were trained on recordings held under research-only terms (the Harvard collection): they are served on this private network only and are never published; releasable adapters were trained on consented or openly licensed recordings only.
