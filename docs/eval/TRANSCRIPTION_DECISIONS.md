| 42 | 2026-09-11 | Legato segmentation for plucked strings (`--legato`; on by default for the oud staff, off for the voice): a note continues through frames the tracker calls unvoiced while the pitch stays within 50 cents of the note and the envelope holds at least 15 percent of the attack; it ends at the next onset, at silence, or at a real pitch change.
