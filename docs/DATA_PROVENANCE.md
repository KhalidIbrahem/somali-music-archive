# Data provenance & rights

Every audio asset and dataset in this project, its source, and its rights
status. Governing rules: provenance is recorded on every archival item;
archival audio is **never committed to git** (the ignore rules block
`apps/web/public/audio/` and `public/demos/audio/`); storage is a private
bucket with signed, expiring URLs and UUID object keys; the platform itself is
invite-only, so archival material is served to registered members, not the
public web.

## 1 · Harvard corpus — Maryan "Aryette" Omar Ali Collection (AWM Spec Coll 103)

- **What:** 124 digitised files / 119 unique tracks (~24 h) of mid-century
  Somali music from the Maryan "Aryette" Omar Ali Collection (*Somali Songs,
  1955–1991*), Archive of World Music, Harvard Loeb Music Library — AWM
  Spec Coll 103. A 105-track MP3 subset is staged locally for research.
- **Canonical source:** each track's `sourceUrl` points at Harvard's copy
  (`nrs.lib.harvard.edu` URNs); the local files are staging copies only.
- **Rights status: NOT rights-cleared.** The recordings in this collection
  are not rights-cleared for redistribution in any form, and **written
  permission from the Loeb Music Library is outstanding**. Until that
  written permission is granted, no recording from this collection may be
  included in any public artifact or demo; corpus items rendered on the
  platform carry the line *"Source: Harvard Loeb Music Library — rights
  unverified"* and are limited to the invite-gated listening programme and
  internal research analysis.
- **⚠ Open exception (2026-08-09) — must be resolved, not papered over:**
  the deployed site currently serves collection audio on the public web in
  two places. (1) The machine-listening demonstration page
  (`/demos/index.html`, publicly reachable) publishes a source-separated
  collection recording — the mixture and four stems. (2) The listening-room
  MP3s under `/audio/…` answer direct URL requests without authentication,
  because the page gate does not extend to static assets, even though the
  `/listen` page itself is invite-only. Both must be removed from public
  serving or moved behind authenticated delivery before the preceding
  paragraph is true of the deployed platform. Listed in "Outstanding
  items" as blocking.
- **Research use:** the corpus was segmented (15 s clips, song-level splits)
  for transcription and model experiments; a manifest with per-file
  `license_status=unknown` is kept alongside the data (`data/manifest.csv`,
  untracked).
- **2026-09-25 completion fetch:** the rest of the collection's linked digital
  objects (975 catalog objects with a digital-content link in the finding-aid
  export; the 105 files from 2025 stay as `data/harvard_raw/legacy/`) is being
  fetched into `data/harvard_raw/` by `apps/ai-service/scripts/harvard_fetch.py`
  through the same public route as the 2025 download (persistent URN → public
  SDS player page → its HLS stream; anonymous session, nothing bypassed,
  login-gated objects skipped), **under the same research-only rule**:
  git-ignored (`data/harvard_raw/`), never served, never uploaded, never in a
  public artifact. The verbatim rights/terms statements on record are in
  `docs/data/HARVARD_TERMS.md`; the fetch accounting (fetched / legacy /
  skipped by reason, the 317 catalog tracks with no digital object, the
  cassette-level objects superseded by their tracks, and the 2025
  whole-cassette files that overlap track files and must stay out of any
  training set that includes those tracks) is `docs/data/HARVARD_GAP_LIST.md`,
  regenerated by `scripts/harvard_gap_report.py`.

## 2 · Beerdilaacshe (the engraved score)

- **Melody:** composed by Abdillahi Qarshe, 1950s.
- **Attribution check (2026-11-08):** one secondary source credits Qarshe
  directly: Geeska, Fathi Jama, "The making of Hudeidi" (20 April 2025), names
  "Ciil, Laac, and Beerdilaacshe, originally composed by Abdullahi Qarshe".
  Jama Musse Jama's *Qaraami* (2023) lists Beerdillaacshe in its publisher
  description with no composer; the book itself was not available. Qarshe's own
  long interview in Geeska, Giannattasio's catalogue and the Wikipedia articles
  do not mention the melody. No source outside this project dates it, so
  "1950s" rests on this record alone. Separately, the singer Cabdi Salaad Aadan
  is known by the nickname Beerdilaacshe (Harvard AWM SC 12217), so a search on
  the title also finds his other work.
- **Composition rights:** Qarshe died in 1994 or 1997, so any life-based term is
  still running, and no permission from his estate is on file. The engraving
  stays on the website as before, but it is **not in the public dataset**:
  `build_hf_dataset.py` leaves it out unless `--include-symbolic` is passed
  (`docs/data/HF_DATASET.md`).
- **Sheet music:** engraved by Khalid Ibrahim; the LilyPond source, PDF, and
  MIDI are committed at `apps/web/public/scores/beerdilaacshe/`.
- **Audio:** there is no archival recording in this feature. The in-browser
  performance is synthesised from the parsed note list (plucked-string oud
  voice + frame-drum pattern, `apps/web/lib/audio/qaraamiEngine.ts`), so the
  playable score and the landing-page demo are the project's own material.

## 3 · Sample transcription session (studio + hero)

The studio's demonstration session (412 notes, 5:48) comes from the project's
own transcription pipeline; its audio is **synthesised from the note list** —
no corpus audio ships in this repository or the app bundle (the deployed
demo page's stems are the separate, blocking exception recorded in §1). The
landing-page hero excerpt is a hand-engraved fixture with synthesised
placeholder audio; the landing page's research-demo player is engine-only
(fully synthesised, no recordings).

## 4 · Oud (kaban) qaraami collection

- **What:** a privately shared collection of oud-led qaraami recordings —
  28 unique files (~8 h) after de-duplication. *(Figure superseded
  2026-09-25 by the inventory: 40 files, 28 unique songs, 5.4 h; see the
  Identification bullet below.)*
- **Rights status:** rights remain with the performers and rights holders.
  Performer names are deliberately **not listed** on the platform; each item
  carries *"Source: private collection — performers unlisted; rights remain
  with their holders."*
- **Use:** nine curated tracks in the members-only listening room (never in
  git; attached at deploy time), and a research dataset (1,921 fifteen-second
  clips: 1,512 train / 133 val / 276 test, song-level splits) for the
  fine-tuning experiments documented in `docs/OUD-LORA-RESULTS.md`. The
  collection itself is not redistributed.
- **Identification (added 2026-09-25):** this collection is **Ahmed
  Ilkacase's** — the 40 files (28 unique songs, 5.4 h) at
  `data/raw_incoming/oud_ilkacase/`, inventoried in
  `docs/data/CORPUS_INVENTORY.md` (per-file records in
  `data/inventory/oud_ilkacase.jsonl`). `CORPUS_INVENTORY.md` and
  `data/inventory/summary.json` carry the earlier label *"Ilkacase Qays"*,
  kept there as the historical name of the source group; the
  performer is Ahmed Ilkacase, who made the recordings and gave them to Khalid
  Ibrahim for this project. Its rights status is the one recorded in §8
  (consented for training; adapters may be published; written consent record
  pending at `docs/data/consent/`). Until that written record exists, the
  platform-facing treatment above (performers unlisted, collection not
  redistributed) stays as it is.

## 5 · Synthetic / model-generated audio

- **Generation-layer outputs** (the /generate feature): produced by
  third-party models under their provider terms; stored under UUID keys in
  the private bucket; labelled "AI-generated · <provider>" in the UI.
- **Model-evaluation outputs** (`data/eval_gen/`, `runs/…/samples/`,
  untracked): 10-second clips generated by base and fine-tuned MusicGen for
  the experiments in `docs/OUD-LORA-RESULTS.md`. Synthetic; derived from
  models trained on the datasets above; research artifacts only.

## 6 · Member-uploaded library documents

The library shelf holds scanned songbooks and sheet-music PDFs uploaded by
members. Rights remain with the uploaders and the original publishers;
files are stored privately (UUID keys) and served via signed URLs to members.

## 7 · Seed / fixture metadata

Development seed recordings use fictional metadata; the singer field reads
"Fanaan (name withheld)". Historical poet attributions that appear
(e.g. Cabdullahi Qarshe) are public-record composer credits.

## 8 · Ahmed Ilkacase session corpus

- **What:** Ahmed Ilkacase's own recording sessions from the old-Mac archive —
  27 Logic projects (`data/ahmed_raw/Logic/<project>/Audio Files/`, stems and
  recorded takes; `Bounces/`, mixdowns) and 178 GarageBand bundles
  (`data/ahmed_raw/GarageBand/<name>.band/Media/`, takes and imports;
  `Output/Output.aif`, the bundle mixdown), plus the separate 40-file
  collection already listed in §4 (`data/raw_incoming/oud_ilkacase/`). Copied
  from the archive volume to `data/ahmed_raw/` and `data/raw_incoming/` on
  2026-09-25; the project bundles (`*.logic/`, `projectData`) are kept for
  provenance only. Project and file names carry the genre (qaraami, balwo,
  heello, dhaanto, buraanbur) and role (cod = vocal take, kaban = oud,
  beat/drum, mix/final) words used to caption the training sets.
- **Who:** Ahmed Ilkacase, musician. He made the recordings and gave them to
  Khalid Ibrahim for this project.
- **Rights status:** consented for training; the resulting adapters may be
  published. **The written consent record is to be signed and its index entry
  added by Khalid at `docs/data/consent/`** (see the README there for what it
  must state, and why the signed document itself stays out of this public
  repository); until
  it exists this section is the only record of the consent and nothing built
  from the corpus is published.
- **Excluded from the training sets:** Apple factory loops shipped with
  Logic/GarageBand (`.caf` such as "80s Dance Bass Synth 07.caf", reason
  `apple_loop`), Logic overview caches (`.ovw`, not audio), and the
  non-qaraami beats / synth / rap material identified in the inventory.
- **Inventory:** `docs/data/AHMED_CORPUS_INVENTORY.md` with the per-file
  records in `data/inventory/ahmed_*.jsonl` (what each file is, its content
  label, and why anything was excluded).
- **Git rule:** the audio is never committed. `.gitignore` covers
  `data/ahmed_raw/`, `data/raw_incoming/`, `data/sets/*/{clips,tokens,chroma}/`
  and every audio extension involved (`*.aif`, `*.aiff`, `*.caf`, `*.m4a`,
  `*.mp3`, `*.wav`, `*.aac`, `*.ovw`, `*.npy`). Only captions, inventories and
  results JSON are tracked.
