## 4. Method

Inventory and labels: `scripts/ahmed_extract.py` walks the GarageBand bundles, Logic projects and the loose collection (ffprobe metadata, full-file sha256 as song identity, silence measure, duplicate and silent exclusions); `scripts/ahmed_classify.py` labels every kept file (MERT-v1-95M embedding, Silero VAD vocal fraction, HPSS percussive ratio, torchcrepe voiced fraction and PCS, spectral flatness) with a logistic regression fitted on name-rule seeds and withholds the label under the probability threshold. Harvard: `scripts/harvard_fetch.py` downloads the public streams of the Aryette finding aid into `data/harvard_raw/` and writes the manifest this report reads; the last manifest row per cid wins. Sets: `scripts/build_sets.py` selects by content label (collection-as-oud rule, third-party exclusion), cuts 30 s clips exactly like `scripts/build_scale_dataset.py` (50 % overlap, peak then -14 LUFS, quiet clips dropped), captions with template v2 and assigns song-group-aware 80/10/10 splits over a split universe, asserting no song_sha256 or song_group in two splits (the leakage column above reruns `find_leakage` on every captions file) and that every stem clip sits in its source mix's split (the stem/mix column reruns `find_stem_split_mismatches` against the mix set's captions). `scripts/scale_tokens.py` writes the EnCodec tokens and `scripts/scale_chroma.py` the musicgen-melody chroma. Separation uses Demucs htdemucs_ft (the oud lands in the `other` stem) with an oud-presence gate; the melody pipeline is CREPE f0 to notes (`f0_notes`, `quantize`, `melody`, `transcribe`). Unique hours count each sha256 once; kept hours are the non-excluded rows; content hours are the classifier's confident labels, everything else is unclassified.

Classifier report: model `LogisticRegression(C=1, class_weight=balanced)`, 490 seed rows, 5-fold CV accuracy 0.786, balanced 0.565; thresholds {"OUD_VAD_MAX": 0.06, "VOICE_VAD_MIN": 0.25, "VOICE_VAD_STRONG": 0.5, "VOICE_VOICED_MIN": 0.6, "VOICE_PERC_MAX": 0.25, "DRUMS_PERC_MIN": 0.6, "DRUMS_VOICED_MAX": 0.2, "SYNTH_FLATNESS_MIN": 0.2, "PROB_MIN": 0.6}.

Library versions (importlib.metadata): torch 2.14.0, transformers 4.57.6, demucs 4.1.0, torchcrepe 0.0.24, peft 0.20.0, librosa 0.11.0.

## 5. What limits quality

Isolated oud is the scarce resource: 4.84 h of recorded oud and no separated stems yet (`data/separated/index.jsonl` or `data/separated/*/*/stems.json` not built yet), against 122.57 h of full mixes (25× more mix than recorded oud); recorded oud is 3 % of the 159.07 h kept. 10.73 h of the 80.18 h with a classifier (13 %) carry no confident content label and enter no set; the classifier's weakest classes (drums, oud, rap, synth) are exactly the ones a set needs. Another 3.22 h are non-qaraami productions (4 %). 94 files (10.24 h) are third-party suspects and are excluded from every published adapter until their rights are settled. Isolated vocals are 5.87 h recorded; a vocal model also needs aligned lyrics, which do not exist yet. Harvard: 408 fetched + 102 legacy objects give 78.89 h kept (164 skipped, 3 of them network errors that a resumed fetch retries, 301 of 975 linked catalog objects not yet attempted). The material is research-only and never served, and no classifier runs on it, so its oud hours exist only through separation (not built yet). No contributor recordings yet; every consented, publishable hour is Ahmed Ilkacase's. Publishable (consented or CC) hours: 80.18; research-only hours: 78.89 (50 % of the kept corpus cannot back a served model). No 10 s sets yet (`data/sets_10s/*/captions.jsonl` not built yet); the 30 s sets are the only training material. The smallest 30 s set is `ahmed_oud` with 1036 clips (8.63 h of overlapping windows) from 37 files: its test split holds 105 clips, so per-song confidence intervals there will be wide.
